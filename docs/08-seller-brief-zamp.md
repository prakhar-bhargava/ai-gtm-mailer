# Seller brief: Zamp (default)

This is the "brief it once" input the pipeline uses to judge relevance and write the value line. Turn it into `config/seller-brief.zamp.json`. Facts below come from docs/research/notes/zamp_company.md; recheck zamp.ai before the interview.

## Who the seller is

Zamp (zamp.ai), founded 2022 by Amit Jain (formerly a Sequoia India partner and head of Uber APAC). Sells autonomous "AI employees" that a company briefs once and that "own the job end to end". Not zamp.com, a different sales-tax company.

## What it sells

AI employees for finance and operations roles. The homepage lists 15+ roles, including Accountant, Procurement, Compliance, RevOps Analyst and GTM Associate. The strongest proof point is invoice processing and accounts payable. Claims on the site: 99%+ accuracy, four-day onboarding, SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, SOX, HIPAA.

## Proof points safe to reference

- Named logos on the site: Uber, DoorDash, Noon, Mindbody, Wio Bank, Sequoia. Careers pages add Stripe.
- Mindbody finance: "Zamp now handles most of our invoice processing end-to-end."

Do not put customer names in the drafts unless the rep turns that on; peer references work (Gong: +88% for same-industry social proof) but a fabricated or mismatched one is worse than none.

## Ideal customer

Enterprises and large mid-market companies with heavy AP, procurement or compliance volume, often on SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Coupa or Ariba. Marketplaces, delivery and SaaS companies with large vendor bases; digital banks and fintechs (GCC and India especially); US mid-market.

Personas: CFO, VP Finance, Controller, Head of AP or Finance Ops, Head of Procurement, compliance and AML leads. CIO as a co-buyer.

## Pains to map signals onto

| Signal | Pain it implies | Zamp angle |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring several AP, AR, accounting or finance ops roles | Volume growing faster than the team | An AI employee takes the repetitive queue so new hires work exceptions |
| ERP migration or new finance system | Process redesign underway; a window to change how work is done | Build the automated flow into the new system from day one |
| Expansion into new countries or entities | More vendors, currencies, tax rules, entities to close | Scale the process without scaling headcount linearly |
| Funding round or growth news | Finance ops must scale for the next stage | Same |
| Exec talks publicly about close speed, automation or AI | Stated priority | Meet them on the priority they named |
| New finance leader in seat (10 to 90 days) | New leaders set a 90 day agenda | Offer a quick win on a known pain |

## Tone

Plain, peer to peer, short. No hype words. One interest question as the CTA.
