# Zamp (zamp.ai): company, product, ICP, AI Solutions role, news, GTM

Research date: 2026-10-03. Sources are mostly Zamp's own website and careers postings (Kula ATS), plus 2022 TechCrunch and a few aggregators. Zamp has little independent press coverage for 2025-2026. Aggregator data (Inc42, BuiltIn, aiindigo) is marked where it conflicts or can't be verified.

**Disambiguation:** "Zamp" (zamp.ai, formerly Varni Labs, earlier on the zamp.finance domain) is a different company from **Zamp at zamp.com**, a US sales-tax compliance startup that raised a $10M Series A led by Valor Equity Partners ([zamp.com press](https://zamp.com/press-media/zamp-raises-10m-series-a-led-by-valor-equity-partners)). Do not mix their funding or customers. The zamp.ai company started in 2022 with a crypto/web3 thesis, so older job boards (web3.career, Polygon ecosystem jobs) still list it.

## 1. What Zamp builds; founding, founders, funding, locations

### Takeaway
Zamp sells autonomous "AI employees" (it also says "digital employees"). Its strongest track record is finance and operations work, especially invoice processing/AP, with banks and Fortune 500 companies. It was founded in 2022 by Amit Jain (ex-Sequoia India partner, ex-Uber APAC head) and raised a seed round of about $22M from Sequoia plus well-known operator angels. The company started out building crypto banking/payments and later pivoted to AI agents.

### Inferences
- "Pace" appears to be the name/persona for Zamp's AI employee, or a consumer-facing character brand. The current homepage, however, says "Hi, I'm Zamp" and never mentions Pace. Reference "Pace" carefully, if at all. The blog's "Outpace Risk" title may be a pun on it.
- The vocabulary to use when framing a build is "AI employee" or "digital employee", "delegate", "owns the job end-to-end", "brief it once", "accountable for the outcome", "move at the speed of thought" and "velocity". Avoid "bot", "copilot" and "automation script". Zamp's homepage explicitly contrasts itself with chatbots and Zapier/UiPath.
- One of the homepage roles is "GTM Associate". A personalised outreach build would fit directly as a demo of Zamp's own "GTM Associate" AI employee.
- The company has broadened from finance ops (2024-25 messaging) to a general "AI employee" platform (2026 homepage). Finance/AP is still where its proof points are.

### Gaps
- No priced round after the 2022 seed was found, and no 2025-2026 funding news. The $22M vs $25M discrepancy is unresolved.
- Co-founders other than Amit Jain were not found.
- I could not confirm whether Pace is the product name or a separate persona/brand initiative.
- HQ: BuiltIn says San Francisco, but the team appears to be mostly in India. No official HQ statement was found.

## 2. Customers / ICP

### Takeaway
Buyers are large enterprises, fintechs/banks and big consumer-tech platforms with heavy finance-ops volume. Named logos are Uber, DoorDash, Stripe, Noon, Mindbody, Wio Bank and Sequoia. Functional buyers appear to be the CFO, controller and AP/finance ops leads, plus CIOs and CISOs who approve deployment, with ERP- and procurement-heavy stacks.

### Cited Findings
- Homepage logos/customers: Sequoia, Noon, DoorDash, Mindbody, Uber, Wio Bank PJSC. — [zamp.ai](https://zamp.ai)
- Testimonial from Yatin Parab, CPO of Wio Bank: "Zamp enables us to securely automate complex operations with precision, scale, and full compliance." — [zamp.ai](https://zamp.ai)
- Testimonial from Kristin Tumulak, Mindbody Finance: "Zamp now handles most of our invoice processing end-to-end." — [zamp.ai](https://zamp.ai)
- Careers copy: "50+ top global organizations and banks (including DoorDash, Uber, and Stripe)". — [Kula: Technical Solutions Engineer](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/25818/)
- The TSE role covers ERPs (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite) and procurement platforms (Coupa, Ariba), handles InfoSec/EARB reviews, talks with CTOs and CISOs, and requires "Fortune 500/Global 2000 buying cycles" experience. — [Kula: TSE](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/25818/)
- The blog covers AML/financial-crime investigations ("Outpace Risk"), CIO-led AI adoption ("Enterprise AI adoption is in the hands of the CIO") and "autonomous companies". — [zamp.ai/blogs](https://www.zamp.ai/blogs)
- A third-party review (2026-09-02) says the target buyers are mid-to-large enterprises with documented workflows and regulated industries, and lists pricing starting around $299/month. — [aiindigo](https://aiindigo.com/blog/zamp-review-2026-the-reality-of-the-autonomous-ai-workforce). **Treat as unreliable:** this is low-quality SEO content, and the pricing contradicts Zamp's enterprise motion with demos and FDEs.

### Inferences
- Realistic demo prospects:
  - Mid-to-large companies with high invoice/AP volume and complex ERP stacks, in the same mould as Mindbody, Noon and DoorDash: marketplaces, delivery/mobility, SaaS with large vendor bases.
  - Digital banks and fintechs in the UAE/GCC and India, like Wio Bank, where AML and compliance ops apply. The investors (Careem, Lean, Noon) point to strong Middle East ties.
  - US mid-market and enterprise companies near the US hubs (Charlotte, Atlanta, Indianapolis, Minneapolis). Charlotte is a banking hub.
- Personas to target: CFO, VP Finance, Controller, Head of AP / Finance Ops, Head of Procurement, CIO, and Head of Compliance/AML.
- Pain hooks: invoice-processing backlog, month-end close, AP headcount, AML alert triage, ERP fragmentation, audit and compliance (SOX).

### Gaps
- There are no detailed public case studies with metrics beyond the testimonials.
- No information on company-size thresholds or official pricing.

## 3. The AI Solutions Associate / Analyst role

### Takeaway
No posting titled "AI Solutions *Associate*" was found. The closest live role is **AI Solutions Analyst** (Founder's Office, Bengaluru/Gurugram, on-site, 0-2 years). The job is to turn customer business requirements into AI-employee workflows, validate and roll them out, and report ROI. It is a solutions/deployment role, not a pure engineering one.

### Cited Findings
- AI Solutions Analyst: full-time, Founder's Office, on-site, Bengaluru and Gurugram. Responsibilities: "Convert customer business requirements into workflow specifications using internal AI tooling"; validate workflows "from design through testing, rollout, and monitoring"; act as the bridge between business teams and product/engineering; support customer adoption and gather feedback; "track performance metrics and communicate ROI to stakeholders." — [Kula: AI Solutions Analyst](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/19526)
- Requirements: 0-2 years of experience; preference for IIT and premier colleges; CS degree strongly preferred with basic Python/JavaScript; structured problem-solving; ownership; cross-functional communication. — [Kula: AI Solutions Analyst](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/19526)
- How to apply: send work samples, not just a résumé, to palak@zamp.ai. — [Kula: AI Solutions Analyst](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/19526)
- The careers index lists no "AI Solutions Associate". Founder's Office openings are Strategy & Operations, AI Solutions Analyst, General Manager and Client Partner. — [Kula careers](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai)
- Related career ladder: the AI Solution Manager (ASM) / General Manager runs a pod P&L as "CEO of your pod", owns the customer lifecycle "from pitch through renewal", leads a 5-6 person pod, and works on-site with customers (US). — [Kula: GM/ASM](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/25234/)
- Forward Deployed Engineers act as "technical co-owners" embedded with customer teams. — [BuiltIn FDE](https://builtin.com/job/forward-deployment-engineer/6298167)

### Inferences
- An "Associate" title is likely a variant of, or the next level above, the Analyst role in the same Founder's Office pod structure (Analyst → Associate? → ASM/GM). This is unverified.
- Framing for the case study: show requirement → workflow spec → test → rollout → metrics/ROI. That mirrors the job description, so the outreach build should include an ROI and measurement layer (reply rate, time saved per SDR) and a human-in-the-loop review step.

### Gaps
- The exact case-study prompt and the Associate-level job description are not publicly findable.

## 5. GTM motion

### Takeaway
Zamp sells enterprise deals: demo-led, with AEs, technical solutions engineers, FDEs and pod GMs who own the cycle "from pitch through renewal". It says it built its own CRM with AI ("vibe-coded"), and it lists "GTM Associate" as one of its AI employees. That is a strong hook for an outreach-automation case study. I found no public post that explicitly describes its outbound playbook.

### Cited Findings
- TSE partners with Account Executives through sales cycles, leads security review through to production approval, and works through multi-stakeholder F500/G2000 buying cycles. — [Kula: TSE](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/25818/)
- GM/ASM owns revenue targets and the lifecycle "from pitch through renewal". — [Kula: GM](https://careers.kula.ai/zamp-ai/25234/)
- The BD & Partnerships Lead role exists (title only, from search). — [BuiltIn Bengaluru](https://builtinbengaluru.in/job/bd-partnerships-lead/2540828)
- Blog: "How We Vibe-Coded Our CRM and Why It Beats Everything Out There" says Zamp built custom agentic CRM infrastructure. — [zamp.ai/blogs](https://www.zamp.ai/blogs)
- Website CTAs are "Hire Me" and "Book Demo", with a four-day onboarding pitch. — [zamp.ai](https://zamp.ai)

### Inferences
- An outreach build that looks like the "GTM Associate" AI employee is the natural fit. It would research a prospect (finance-ops pain, ERP stack, recent news), write a personalised first-person note, and log to a CRM. The four-day "brief once, runs end-to-end" framing also fits.
- Founder-network outbound is likely, given the operator-angel investors (Uber, DoorDash, Careem, Nium), but this is unverified.

### Gaps
